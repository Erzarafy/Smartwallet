HAVE QUESTIONS?

Quick answers,
more confidence.

Need more help? Smart AI is here to answer questions about your finances.

What is SmartWallet?+

SmartWallet is a personal cash flow app that helps you track transactions, review your finances, and make a plan.

Can SmartWallet be used by multiple people?+

Yes. Each person can use their own account and manage their financial records separately.

What can I ask the AI chatbot?+

Ask about your cash flow summary or spending habits. Use AI to help understand your records, not as a replacement for professional financial advice.

Do I need to be good with money to get started?+

Not at all. SmartWallet is designed to be easy to understand. Start by tracking everyday transactions and explore your summaries at your own pace.
